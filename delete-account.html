Account and Data Deletion

Smart Diet Advisor, published by MJ Barki Studio. Last updated: 24 November 2026

You can delete your Smart Diet Advisor account and all of its associated data at any time. This page explains how.

Delete your account from the app

  1. Open the Smart Diet Advisor app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

This permanently deletes your account and data. The action cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app

If you are locked out or cannot sign in, email us at mjbarki.studio@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account and ask us to delete your account. We will verify your request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address);
  • Your profile information (biological sex, age, height, weight, target weight, activity level, goal, and calculated targets);
  • Your meal and nutrition history.

Your meal photos are never stored at any point, so there is nothing to delete there.

What may be retained

We do not keep your personal data after deletion. Anonymous crash and diagnostic logs (which do not include your health data or identify you) may persist for a limited period under our crash-reporting provider's standard retention before being removed automatically.
