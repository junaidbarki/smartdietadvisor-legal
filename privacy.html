Privacy Policy — Smart Diet Advisor

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MJ Barki Studio ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Smart Diet Advisor mobile application ("the App").

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

Smart Diet Advisor is published by MJ Barki Studio. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at: mjbarki.studio@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

We collect only what the App needs to work:

a) Account information — When you create an account, we collect your email address (and, if you sign in with Google, the basic account information Google provides). Authentication is handled through our service provider, Supabase.

b) Health and profile information — To calculate your personalized nutrition targets, we collect your biological sex, age, height, current weight, target weight, activity level, and goal.

c) Meal and nutrition data — When you analyze or log a meal, we store the results of that analysis (food names, estimated calories, macronutrients, micronutrients), along with the meal type and date/time, so you can view it in your history.

d) Meal photos — When you take or select a photo of a meal, the image is sent to our processing service for analysis. We do not store your meal photos. The photo is used only to generate the nutrition estimate and is not retained after processing.

e) Activity data — If you grant permission, the App reads your device's step count to show an on-device estimate of calories burned. This step data is processed on your device and is not stored on our servers or transmitted to us.

f) Diagnostic information — We use crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) which may collect device model, OS version, and crash logs. We configure it so that it does not include your health data.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or any data beyond what is described here.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account and keep you signed in;
  • Calculate your personalized daily calorie and macronutrient targets;
  • Analyze the meals you photograph or enter, and show you the results;
  • Save your meal history so you can review it;
  • Generate reports you choose to export;
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the App's stability.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. How your information is processed and shared

Your data is handled by a small number of trusted service providers who process it on our behalf:

  • Supabase — stores your account, profile, and meal history, and manages sign-in. (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Google (Gemini AI) — processes the content you submit for meal analysis to generate nutrition estimates. (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • USDA FoodData Central — a public nutrition database we query to verify nutrition values. We send food names for lookup; we do not send your personal information to USDA.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — processes diagnostic/crash information.

These providers may store and process data on servers located outside your country. We only share the minimum information necessary for each provider to perform its function. We do not otherwise share, rent, or sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or the safety of others.

5. Data security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and access to your account data is protected by authentication and database security rules. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and meal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your associated personal data as described below. Meal photos are not retained at any point.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and update your profile information at any time within the App.
  • Delete your account and data — You can permanently delete your account and associated data from within the App (Profile → Delete account). This removes your profile and meal history from our systems. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Withdraw permissions (such as activity/step access) through your device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing, and the right to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at mjbarki.studio@gmail.com.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Medical disclaimer

Smart Diet Advisor provides general wellness and nutrition information only. It is not medical advice, and the nutrition values it provides are estimates. It is not a substitute for professional medical, dietary, or health advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions about your diet or health.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date. If we make significant changes, we will take reasonable steps to notify you. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
